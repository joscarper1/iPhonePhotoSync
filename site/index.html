What it does

Made for people who want their memories safe and the confidence to free up space on their iPhone.

🔒

Read-only

Reads /DCIM over USB. It never writes, moves or deletes anything on the iPhone. No recompression or conversion (HEIC, JPEG, PNG, MOV, MP4, Live Photos and .AAE).

✅

SHA-256 verification

Atomic copy (temp file → rename), integrity check, and it never overwrites an existing file.

🔁

Incremental and resumable

Detects what is still missing by comparing with your previous backup and existing library. If interrupted, it continues without repeating what was already copied.

🗂️

Automatic organization

Album › Year › Month › Place, using capture date, GPS (geocoding without Internet) and your iPhone albums.

🖼️

Thumbnail gallery

Review what is pending and choose what to back up before copying.

🧾

Clear report

Tells you what was copied, what wasn't and why, and generates the list of what you should not delete from the iPhone.

How to use it

Three steps from the graphical interface.

  1. Connect and trust. Plug in the iPhone over USB, unlock it and tap Trust.
  2. Scan. Choose the destination disk (the folder that contains iPhone_Photos) and click Scan iPhone.
  3. Back up. Review what is pending, tick what you want and click Back up.

Also from the command line

python -m pip install -e ".[dev]"
python tools/fetch_exiftool.py
iphonephotosync-ui          # GUI
iphonephotosync --help      # scan, preview, backup,
                            # verify, status,
                            # deletion-report

Privacy: nothing leaves your computer

No photos, videos, metadata or coordinates are sent to any service.

Local geocoding

Uses a local database (GeoNames), without querying servers.

Thumbnails on your disk

Stored only in _Manifest/thumbs at your destination.

Minimal reading

From the iPhone Photos database only albums and the favorite flag are read; the temporary copy is deleted.

Frequently asked questions

 

Can I delete photos from the iPhone after backing up?

The tool generates a list of what you should not delete. Read the guide to freeing up space (in Spanish) before doing so.

Are the originals of edited photos backed up?

Not entirely: unedited originals live in /PhotoData/Mutations and are not backed up. The visible version in /DCIM plus its .AAE file is copied; the report marks them as "not safe".

The iPhone stops responding after thousands of files

The file service (AFC) may answer NO_RESOURCES and lock up until you reconnect or restart the phone. The tool detects it, stops the session cleanly and you can resume. Quick scan and the metadata cache reduce it.

What speed can I expect?

About 30 MB/s measured over USB; it depends on the iPhone, not on the disk.

Does it reorganize my existing library?

No. Only new items use the album structure. Albums are also not recognized if Apple changes the internal schema in an iOS version; in that case it continues without albums and warns you.

Is it paid?

No. It is free and open source (GPL-3.0-or-later). If you find it useful you can make a voluntary contribution.
